# About Admire Madyira

Admire Theophilus Madyira is a Software Engineer at Microsoft, based in the
Greater Seattle Area (Redmond, WA).

## What he works on

At Microsoft he works on Build, Release, Update, and Deployment infrastructure
for Copilot and Microsoft Edge. His work includes engineering AI agents
integrated into the Copilot and Edge release pipelines, monitoring build health
and fleet telemetry, and owning deployment infrastructure end to end.

Earlier he worked on search optimization and browser latency improvement using
C++ for the Edge browser, and on applied machine learning research into Copilot
safety and robustness to adversarial attacks such as prompt injection.

## Outside of work

Admire cares deeply about educational equity and has spent his time and skills
trying to improve access to education in his communities. He also loves playing
soccer.
